# Biped helper-combination bug hunt

No new SDK defects were reproduced in the armed 2 m biped scenario. Existing `SURFACE-2` (drape paths retain only their first hit's bone) was deliberately exercised but is already reported in `scratch/astra-reports/hunt-surface.md`; this scenario's strap stayed within 18.63 mm average of the posed torso surface (versus 17.16 mm before posing), so it adds no distinct consequence.

## Covered

- `scratch/hunt-biped/hunt-biped-orc.ts` is a docs-shaped armed orc: a lofted torso over a spine chain, bilateral `limb`-driven legs and arms, palm plus named finger/thumb digit chains, a helmet lathe, belt from `surface.loop`, diagonal clothing strap from `surface.drape`, chest and jaw decals, and a sword on its own `swordGrip` hinge bone. `npm run typecheck -- scratch/hunt-biped/hunt-biped-orc.ts` passed with no type errors.
- `scratch/hunt-biped/hunt-biped-probe.ts` — `npx tsx scratch/hunt-biped/hunt-biped-probe.ts` built the sample, posed the chest 22° and right elbow 35°, then read all handles again. All decal vertices stayed finite. The chest decal centroid moved `[0,1.340208,0.228885] → [0.008062,1.341775,0.228885]`; the jaw decal moved `[0.000048,1.768991,0.264035] → [-0.130690,1.743597,0.264035]`, consistent with their respective posed bones and explicit outward `dir: [0,0,1]`.
- The weapon's own-bone attachment stayed coincident with the hand bone: hand-to-`swordGrip` gap was `2.62e-16 m` both before and after posing. The sword's `weaponGrip`, `weaponGuard`, and `weaponBlade` all carried `swordGrip` 100%.
- The preview probe `npm run preview -- scratch/hunt-biped/hunt-biped-orc.ts --shot --gap weaponGrip handR --bones swordGrip` succeeded. It reported `Gap weaponGrip ↔ handR: overlapping` and `Bones of swordGrip: swordGrip 100%`; the final model had 23 parts and 1,832 triangles.
- The belt remained surface-seated after posing: average mesh-vertex distance to the refreshed torso surface was `20.09 mm` before and `20.42 mm` after. Its centroid moved `11.28 mm`; the draped strap's centroid moved `39.86 mm` while its average surface distance changed only `17.16 → 18.63 mm`.
- The rig answer key contained left/right arm and leg chains and four digit chains with stable names: `fingerL1/fingerL2/fingerLTip`, `thumbL`, `fingerR1/fingerR2/fingerRTip`, and `thumbR`; leg contacts were `[0.2,0.055,0.16]` and `[-0.2,0.055,0.16]`. The spine chain correctly remained side `C`.
- Both leg contacts reached the floor without sinking: preview's lowest bound was `y = 0.0004 m` on `legR`; the direct probe measured `rootMinY = 0.003414 m`. Both boot bounds were finite (`min y = 0.020 m`).
- The first draft intentionally exposed two authoring traps and was corrected before conclusions: weapon `at` values must be model-space points (using `weaponBone.local(...)` derives them correctly), and a weapon bone with world-vertical geometry must use `dir: [0,1,0]` or an explicit frame. These are documented placement/orientation rules, not SDK defects.
