# Styled helper-combination bug hunt

No new bugs isolated in the styled kit combinations exercised here. Existing texture/decal findings were not duplicated: the warm-grey tint classification and missed-ray slivers remain TEXDECAL-1/TEXDECAL-2, and decal edge smoothing remains TEXDECAL-3.

## Covered

- `scratch/hunt-styled/hunt-styled-pixel.ts` — `npm run typecheck -- scratch/hunt-styled/hunt-styled-pixel.ts` passed; `npm run preview -- scratch/hunt-styled/hunt-styled-pixel.ts` completed with 4 parts / 62 triangles and wrote four texture outputs (`svg-head-box-head-64x16.png`, `svg-head-plane-head-8x8.png`, `svg-root-box-root-16x16.png`, and `paint-sheet-1024x884.png`). This exercised `pixelArt`, `pixelTexture`, palette ramps, `tileBox` with seeded crop offsets, `skinLayout`/`skinBox`, grey texture tinting, and `cellPaint` on a faceted sweep.
- `scratch/hunt-styled/hunt-styled-toon.ts` — typecheck and preview passed; preview completed with 6 parts / 454 triangles and wrote a shared `animeEye` decal texture plus the SVG badge and paint sheet. This exercised `cel`, `furEdge`, `inkLines`, `animeEye`, mirrored paired decals, SVG tinting, and cards reusing the same drawn texture.
- `scratch/hunt-styled/hunt-styled-flat.ts` — typecheck and preview passed; preview completed with 7 parts / 144 triangles. Node normal probe reported: `part` 36 vertices / 6 normals / non-indexed, `sweep smooth:false` 96 / 10 / non-indexed, `extrude` 36 / 6 / non-indexed, `lathe segments:8` 192 / 11 / non-indexed, planar `decal` 9 / 1 / indexed, and `cards` 4 / 1 / indexed. The decal crossed no sharp target edge in this ordinary probe; the existing TEXDECAL-3 edge-shading finding therefore remains the applicable known limitation.
- `scratch/hunt-styled/hunt-styled-density.ts` — `npx tsx ...` passed. `tileBox` and `skinBox` mapped exact `.02 m` pixels on faces where the source sheet had enough pixels. A deliberately undersized 16×4 tile enlarged one 0.16×0.12 m face to 0.03 m/pixel, matching the kit's documented “face larger than tile shows the whole tile with larger texels” caveat; no inconsistency beyond that documented fallback was found.
- `scratch/hunt-styled/hunt-styled-atlas.ts` — `npx tsx ...` passed. Two separately-created identical pixel textures reused one atlas tile (`packed: 3`, both `{x:136,y:8,w:8,h:8}`); the tinted grayscale tile and coloured SVG stayed separate, and the presence of a pixelated source correctly selected nearest atlas magnification. No unexpected atlas growth or tile-reuse failure was observed.
- The 64-colour/material boundary was not re-reported: `hunt-builder` already covered 65 flat colours and established that the SDK intentionally leaves lab-budget enforcement to the author (docs/api.md:80).
