# Mechanical helper hunt

No new mechanical-area SDK defects were reproduced in the combined hard-surface scenarios below. Existing findings were skipped: `TOOLING-1`/`DOCS-4` cover the hyphenated scratch-path failure in `snap --report-only`; `OUTLINE-1` covers degenerate axis-touching lathe triangles; and `KITS-1` covers `clockwork.coil` endpoint offsets.

## Covered

- **Articulated robot arm:** `scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-robot.ts` typechecks with `npm run typecheck -- scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-robot.ts` and previews with `npm run preview -- scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-robot.ts --shot --gap upperArmPlate foreArmPlate --box wristGear`. The preview reports 9 named parts / 1,072 triangles, resolves the requested gap as 99.3 mm, and resolves the requested `wristGear` box (111.9 × 12.0 × 111.9 mm). The robot combines hinge joints, painted extrudes, a painted 8-segment lathe barrel, flat `part` geometry, `clockwork.gearTrain`, and `gearTrain.spin(12)` without an assembly error.
- **Hinge posing:** `scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-pose.ts` — `npx tsx scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-pose.ts`. Posing the elbow 45° about a line along world +X moves the wrist from `[0, 2.28, 0]` to `[0, 2.0954772721, 0.4454772721]`; expected rotation gives `[-5.1e-18, 2.0954772721, 0.4454772721]`, error `5.57e-17 m`. The rigid forearm part moves from `[0,1.95,0]` to `[0,1.8621320344,0.2121320344]` with no invalid skin data. The generated rig includes hinge records for the posed joints.
- **Outline pieces through a hinge:** `scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-outline-pose.ts` — `npx tsx scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-outline-pose.ts`. A painted extrude plate and painted lathe drum remain in the scene (`meshCount: 2`, names `plate`, `drum`) and their frame orientations update under a 90° hinge pose: plate axis changes from approximately `[0,0,1]` to `[0,-1,0]`, while the drum's +Y-facing axis remains along its X axle as expected.
- **Wheeled cart:** `scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-cart.ts` typechecks cleanly and previews with `npm run preview -- scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-cart.ts --shot --gap wheelLeftFront wheelRightFront --box cartChassis`. It reports 11 named parts / 644 triangles; all four 10-segment lathe wheels and their axles are present, the requested wheel gap is 979.9 mm, and `cartChassis` resolves to 1200.0 × 180.0 × 720.0 mm. A direct rigged harness assembly (`hunt-mechanical-assemble-rigged-all.ts`) reports 5 joints, 11 parts, 644 triangles, and only the intentional 42 mm floor-clearance warning.
- **Plain scenery with one root joint:** `scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-diorama.ts` previews with `npm run preview -- scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-diorama.ts --shot --gap glowingLamp glowingBeacon --box glowingLamp`. It reports 6 named parts / 164 triangles, root bone `scene` for every part, a 410.5 mm named gap, and the requested lamp box as 240.4 × 340.6 × 240.4 mm. Direct `harness/assemble(root, true)` in `hunt-mechanical-assemble-rigged.ts` succeeds with one root joint, six parts, no issues, and bounds `[-1.2,0,-0.8]..[1.2,1.0012,0.8]`. Static `assemble(root, false)` gives the old documented root-only/static-lane warning (`1 joints ignored: arm A exports a bare mesh`), not a new mechanical defect; `snap` would choose rigged lane B because the root joint is present.
- **Glow survival:** `scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-materials.ts` — `npx tsx scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-materials.ts`. `glowingLamp` has emissive `ffb347`, intensity `2.2`; `glowingBeacon` has emissive `4fe3d0`, intensity `1.4`; all hard-surface parts have finite normals and flat parts have split position/normal counts (e.g. lamp 96/96, beacon 240/240). Direct harness assembly produces two glow materials (strengths 1.4 and 2.2) and no assembly issues in rigged mode.
- **Snap path check:** `npm run snap -- scratch/hunt-mechanical/hunt-mechanical-diorama.ts --report-only` exits before importing with `Missing or invalid <slug>`, the already-reported hyphenated-path tooling defect (`TOOLING-1` / `DOCS-4`), so no new mechanical conclusion is attached to it.
