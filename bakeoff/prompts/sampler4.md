## Samplers

At the end of this prompt are four samplers picked for this request: a truck (a pickup with several constructions of its cab, bed, wheels, suspension, bumpers, lights, grilles, mirrors, exhausts and racks), a tank's tracks and armour, mounted weapons, and sci-fi hull detail (thrusters, vents, antennas, domes, panels, light bars, cables). They show what vehicles like this carry and how the SDK builds hard shapes.

The pitch is the subject and the base. Imagine the vehicle it describes: who drives it, what it is for, what it has been through, and build rich ideas on top of it. Everything is your own design and construction: body, proportions, signature features and every detail. Where a sampler sparks an idea, take the idea, then build it new and invent more detail on top of it, as the level-of-detail section asks.
