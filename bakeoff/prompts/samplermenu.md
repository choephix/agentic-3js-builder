## Sampler menus

At the end of this prompt are the menus of samplers picked for this request, from vehicles of this kind (a pickup and its variants, armour, mounted weapons, sci-fi hull detail, 2077 paints and neon): the parts they carry and different ways to build each, one line apiece.

The pitch is the subject. Imagine the vehicle it describes: who drives it, what it is for, what it has been through. Build that vehicle rich, your own design and your own construction throughout: body, proportions, signature features and the intricate details on top. The menus are there to spark ideas; every line of the module is new.
