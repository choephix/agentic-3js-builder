## How to build

Build with the SDK throughout. Its helpers are the tested way to get bodies that bend smoothly with the rig and details seated exactly on curved surfaces: chains and sweeps or lofts for bodies, necks, tails and limbs; `surface` hits with `stick`, `ring` and `along` for everything placed on a body; extrudes, lathes and membranes for flat, turned and webbed shapes. Plain three.js geometry fits the simplest pieces.
