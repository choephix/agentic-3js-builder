# Game asset brief

You are building a game-ready asset for a real-time game. Players see it at gameplay distance, from a few angles, among many other assets.

- Decide quickly and write. The brief carries the design; make each choice once and move on.
- Spend the detail where a player looks: the silhouette, the face if there is one, and the subject's one or two signature features. Make those rich: layered shapes, trims, painted markings, small accessories.
- Everything else gets simple, clean shapes with good colour and a texture: backs, undersides, interiors, filler.
- Use a vibrant, readable palette with strong contrast between the main masses.
- The subject fills its bounds. Add a base only when the brief names one.
