## The target

The finished models people single out in this library share a profile: 180–340 parts, 5–12 textures, and modules of 700–1,100 lines. The textures are where the character lives: SVG drawings for eyes, faces, emblems, runes, labels, patterned cloth and trim, and paints for fur, wood, stone, metal and skin. Lanterns, fire, crystals, magic and screens glow. The subject stands on a small staged base or setting that frames it and tells its story, and its style is unmistakable from the first glance.

Decide the parts and the textures, then write the whole module: it is the deliverable, so give it the length the model needs.
