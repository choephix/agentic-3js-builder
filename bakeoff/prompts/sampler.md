## Samplers

At the end of this prompt are samplers picked for this request: working recipe files for vehicles of this kind. One holds a finished pickup with several named constructions of each of its parts (cabs, beds, wheels, bumpers, grilles); the others hold track units and armour, mounted weapons, sci-fi hull detail, and 2077 colour schemes, paints, neon, decals and screens. Every recipe renders, uses the SDK the way it is meant, and mounts by one convention, so it is a fast, safe way to get a hard part right.

The pitch is the subject, and the samplers are the ground you build it on. Start from the pitch: imagine the vehicle it describes, who drives it, what it is for, what it has been through, and give it the silhouette, proportions and stand-out features that story calls for. Build those ideas rich: the features the pitch names, and two or three of your own that make this vehicle unlike any other. That vehicle is the deliverable; the samplers serve it.

Take recipes where they serve your design, copy them into your module (it imports only `three` and the SDK) and make each one yours: reshape it, rescale it, push it further, combine two into one. The body, the proportions and the signature features are your own construction, drawn from the pitch. A viewer who knows the sampler pickup sees a new vehicle, with the sampler's craft in its details.
