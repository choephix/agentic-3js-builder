## The target

The finished models people single out in this library share a profile: 180–340 parts and modules of 700–1,100 lines. The geometry is where the character lives: eyes, faces, emblems, runes, labels, patterned cloth and trim built as parts of their own, and a flat colour per part for fur, wood, stone, metal and skin. Lanterns, fire, crystals, magic and screens glow. The subject is the whole deliverable: ground, rocks, props or scenery appear only where the brief names them, sized to the subject, and its style is unmistakable from the first glance.

Decide the parts and their colours, then write the whole module: it is the deliverable, so give it the length the model needs.
