## The target

The finished models people single out in this library share a profile: 90–170 parts, 2.5–6 textures, and modules of 350–550 lines. The textures are where the character lives: SVG drawings for eyes, faces, emblems, runes, labels, patterned cloth and trim, and paints for fur, wood, stone, metal and skin. Lanterns, fire, crystals, magic and screens glow. The subject is the whole deliverable: ground, rocks, props or scenery appear only where the brief names them, sized to the subject, and its style is unmistakable from the first glance.

Decide the parts and the textures, then write the whole module: it is the deliverable, so give it the length the model needs.
