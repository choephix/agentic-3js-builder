Before any code, write the build sheet for this model, in plain words and numbers:

1. **Identity.** The two or three cues that make the subject read instantly as itself, and how big each is built.
2. **Proportions.** The main masses with their sizes in meters, pushed the way the style pushes them, and the scale constant for each feature (head, hands, signature feature).
3. **Face.** Eyes, muzzle or mouth, ears and brows: shapes, sizes, positions relative to the head, and what keeps them clear from the front.
4. **Signature features.** How each is constructed: blades, cards, assemblies, layers.
5. **Surfaces.** Each paint and each drawing, what it shows and where it goes.
6. **Setting.** The base footprint against the subject's, and the few props with their places.
7. **Budget.** Rough part count per feature, adding up to the target.

Reply with the build sheet only.
